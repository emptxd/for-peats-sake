Success Stories

Henning Voigt — Malchin, Germany

Henning Voigt is a young farmer managing a rewetted fen near Malchin in northeast Germany. Collaborating with the Greifswald Mire Centre, he cultivates cattail (Typha) on former grazing land.

Aldert Van Weeren — Eilanden, Netherlands

Aldert Van Weeren is a Dutch farmer and entrepreneur near Amsterdam, specializing in cultivating Typha (cattail). Beyond farming, he actively promotes paludiculture through policy advocacy and international collaborations, including guiding study tours for organizations like the UK’s Great Fen project.

Ralf Betge — Benz, Germany

Ralf Betge, based in Benz on Usedom Island, is a reed harvester and processor. Transitioning from traditional thatching, he now manages approximately 70 hectares of wet land and harvests reed beds, supplying local materials for roofing and contributing to sustainable land use.

Regan Family — Gortavally, Ireland

The Regan family operates Ireland’s first on-farm paludiculture trial at their farm in Gortavally. As part of the “Peatlands for Prosperity” project by Green Restoration Ireland, they cultivate wetland crops on rewetted peatlands, aiming to demonstrate sustainable agricultural practices that preserve peat soils and reduce CO₂ emissions.
